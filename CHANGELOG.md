# Changelog


## 1.3.2

- Releases are signed with the project's own key, so each new version installs over the last one (uninstall a 1.3 or 1.3.1 download once first).

## 1.3.1

- Releases: the APK is built and attached to each GitHub release automatically.

## 1.3 (November 2026)

- **Moving apps, the Google TV way**: hold OK on an app in the row and it lifts; Left/Right
  carry it along while the others slide aside; OK puts it down, Back puts it back, Down asks to
  hide it. Settings → Apps works the same way (OK picks an app up, the arrows move it), with
  Hidden apps shown / not shown and Reset order. The Move left · Move right menu is gone.
- The apps row shows a newly installed or updated app (and its new banner) straight away.

## 1.2 (November 2026)

- **Apps**: choose the apps in the row and their order (Settings → Apps: Move earlier, Move
  later, To the front, Hide or Show), or hold OK on an app on the countdown (Move left · Move
  right · Hide).
- **A new icon**: two glowing green eyes in the dark, DOOMSDAY in gold on the banner (drawn in
  code: `doomsday/icons/`).
- **Home on first start**: the app asks once "Make Doomsday your home screen?" and shows how
  (Google TV: two steps in Accessibility; boxes with a home choice: the choice; Fire TV may block
  it). It's offered as a home app from the start. Not asked when another app already has Home.
- The card for a missing picture steps aside while the apps are up.

## 1.1 (November 2026)

- City: **Automatic** finds your town from your internet connection (once a day), or **Search
  for a town** finds any town in the world with the app's keyboard (now with a space key).
  The fixed list of cities is gone.
- **Home button**: open Doomsday when Home is pressed. On Google TV through Accessibility (you
  switch it on); on Android TV boxes with a home choice through "Offer as home app". Fire TV may
  block it. Doomsday leaves Home alone when another app already handles it.

## 1.0 (November 2026)

- The countdown to Avengers: Doomsday (UK, Wednesday 16 December 2026) in the film's style:
  months, days, hours, minutes and seconds, rolling digits, a glow on each second. On the day:
  "Avengers: Doomsday is here".
- Today's date, the clock and the weather (Open-Meteo) along the top.
- Four picture slots (Hall, Mask, Tapestry, Throne) and Rotate. Pictures by link, downloaded
  only when you press Download, kept on the TV.
- The app's own on-screen keyboard, starting from `https://tinyurl.com/`.
- Living pictures: breathing light, mist, dust, a slow camera move, green energy that leaps to
  the selected button. Summon on a two-second hold of OK.
- The TV's apps in a row above the buttons.
- Settings: rotate interval, clock, weather and city, Full or Calm effects, dim at night,
  ticking sound, screensaver.
- Screensaver on Google TV / Android TV. Works on Amazon Fire TV (no screensaver there).
