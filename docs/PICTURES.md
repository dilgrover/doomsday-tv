# Pictures

The app has four slots: **Hall, Mask, Tapestry and Throne** (the names are just labels; any
picture can go in any slot). Each slot takes **a link to a picture**. Nothing comes with the
app, and nothing downloads until you press **Download**. Downloaded pictures are kept in the
app's own storage on your TV, and **Remove** deletes them.

## The link must open the picture itself

Open the link in a browser first. If you see **only the picture** (or it starts downloading),
the TV can use it. If you see a web page (a gallery, a sign-in page, a Drive or Dropbox
preview), the TV will get that page instead and say it isn't a picture.

The app accepts `https` links to JPEG, PNG or WebP pictures up to 25 MB, follows short links
to where they lead, and shows that site's name before you download.

## From Google Drive

1. Upload the picture. Right-click → **Share** → General access: **Anyone with the link**
   (Viewer).
2. Copy the link. It looks like
   `https://drive.google.com/file/d/FILE_ID/view?usp=sharing`.
3. Make it a direct link with the `FILE_ID` part:
   `https://drive.google.com/uc?export=download&id=FILE_ID`

Tip: share a whole folder as *Anyone with the link* once, and every picture in it follows.

## From Dropbox

**Share → Copy link**, then change the `dl=0` at the end to **`dl=1`**.

## Short links (typing less)

Long links are slow to type with a remote. Make a short link at [tinyurl.com](https://tinyurl.com)
(you can choose your own ending, for example `tinyurl.com/my-hall`). In the app the box already
starts with `https://tinyurl.com/`, so you type only `my-hall`. Typing just the code works too.
The keyboard also has a `tinyurl.com/` key, and the chips above each box switch to `bit.ly/`
or a full link.

## What makes a good picture

- **Wide and large**: 16:9, 1920×1080 or bigger.
- **Dark on the left**, where the countdown sits.
- **Something bright in the middle**: windows, torches, a glowing floor, light from above. The
  app lifts the picture's own bright parts out and makes them breathe.

A few pictures are recognised by their fingerprint and get effects placed exactly on them
(glowing eyes, fire in the hands, a floor circle that lights up). Every other picture gets the
rest: the light, mist, dust, the camera move, the energy jumps and Summon.
